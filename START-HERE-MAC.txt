================================================================
  JOB APPLIER  —  Start here (Mac)
================================================================

WHAT IT IS
  A little app that finds UK legal jobs, scores how well they fit
  you, and writes a tailored CV + cover letter for each one — so
  applying takes a couple of minutes instead of twenty.


HOW TO START IT
  1. Double-click the file:  start-mac.command
  2. The first time, it sets itself up (a few minutes). After that
     it's quick.
  3. Your web browser opens to the app automatically.
  4. Leave the little black "Terminal" window open while you use it.

  To STOP the app: just close that black window.


FIRST TIME: IF DOUBLE-CLICKING DOESN'T WORK
  Mac may block files it didn't download itself. Do this once:
    • Right-click (or Control-click) start-mac.command
    • Choose "Open"
    • Click "Open" again on the warning.
  From then on, a normal double-click works.

  Still stuck? Open the "Terminal" app (find it with Spotlight: press
  Cmd-Space, type Terminal), then type:  bash
  (with a space after it), drag the start-mac.command file into the
  Terminal window, and press Return.


USING IT  (three tabs in the browser)
  1. Your Profile  — fill in your details once, click Save.
  2. Find Jobs     — it searches automatically and prepares your
                     best matches.
  3. Review & Apply— open each job, copy the cover letter + CV,
                     paste into their form, submit, mark as applied.


GET LOTS MORE JOBS  (free, 2 minutes — optional but recommended)
  1. Go to  https://www.reed.co.uk/developers  and sign up (free).
  2. Create an API key and copy it.
  3. Open the file named  .env  in this folder (TextEdit is fine).
  4. Put your key after REED_API_KEY=  so it looks like:
       REED_API_KEY=your-key-here
  5. Save, then stop and re-start the app.


YOUR PRIVACY
  Everything stays on this Mac. Your details and the jobs are
  saved only here — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  Full details are in GUIDE.md.
